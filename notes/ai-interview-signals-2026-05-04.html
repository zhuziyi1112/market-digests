Candidate Questions to Add

  • vLLM adds DeepSeek V4 stabilization in a patch release. What tests would you run before upgrading a production inference cluster?
  • A provider retires a 1M-context beta header. How do you migrate long-context customers without breaking workflows?
  • Why can a model be “supported” by a serving framework but still not production-ready?
  • How do model deprecations and preview-model shutdowns change your model registry, routing, and eval strategy?
  • In GraphRAG, how do schema constraints and token usage tracking improve reliability and operational cost control?
  • A vector DB release fixes TTL, cache, and export behavior. Why should an AI engineer care if retrieval metrics look unchanged?
