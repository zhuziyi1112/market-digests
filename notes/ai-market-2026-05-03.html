Investment Implications

  • Positive for control-plane vendors: governance, identity, session management, audit logs, connectors, and eval tooling become core enterprise buying criteria.
  • Positive for framework/runtime vendors: harness profiles and remote runtimes create differentiation beyond model access.
  • Watch eval spend: expensive noisy evals may slow adoption unless platforms offer reliable low-cost regression paths.
  • Open-weight pressure persists: Mistral’s Medium 3.5 self-hosting story may pressure proprietary providers in regulated or cost-sensitive accounts.
