Part 2：Workplace English Drill

Scenario

你收到一个模糊需求，需要澄清目标、优先级和完成标准。

Useful phrases

  • Could you clarify the main objective?
  • What does success look like for this?
  • Which part is the highest priority?
  • Are there any constraints I should be aware of?
  • Let me repeat my understanding...

Standard response

```text

Before I start, could I clarify the main objective and what success looks like? My understanding is that the priority is to identify the top risks, not to build a full report yet. Is that right? Also, are there any constraints around timeline, audience, or format that I should keep in mind?

```

Part 3：AI / Data Science Communication

Scenario

把一个业务问题转成 data science problem：客户流失增加，团队想知道为什么。

Useful phrases

  • I would first translate the business question into a measurable outcome.
  • The target variable would be...
  • We need to define the observation window and prediction window.
  • I would start with an exploratory analysis before building a model.

Standard explanation

```text

I would first translate the business question into a measurable outcome. In this case, the target could be whether a customer churns within the next 30 or 60 days. Then I would define the observation window, collect relevant behavioral and account features, and start with exploratory analysis to identify major patterns. Only after that would I decide whether we need a predictive model, a segmentation analysis, or a root-cause analysis.

```

Your turn

用英文解释 45-60 秒：

Rewrite Practice

把下面中式/生硬表达改自然：

  1. Your requirement is not clear.
    • Better: I want to make sure I understand the requirement correctly before moving forward.
    • I don't understand what you want.
    • Better: Could you clarify the main objective and what success looks like?
    • We can use machine learning to solve churn.
    • Better: I’d first define churn clearly, identify the prediction window, and validate whether a model is the right approach.

Stakeholder Versions

同一个 AI/Data point，对不同听众表达：

Engineer

I’d state the metric movement, validate it by segment, and check whether the result is statistically and practically significant.

PM

The result is promising, but we need to confirm whether it improves the user or business outcome we care about.

Executive

The signal is useful, but I would recommend a limited rollout with monitoring before making it a full launch.
