Investment Implications

  • Positive for infra/control-plane vendors: model registry, routing, eval migration, deprecation monitoring, and serving validation look increasingly mandatory.
  • Positive for open-model ecosystems only when serving stack matures: DeepSeek-style models depend on vLLM/serving runtime readiness, not just benchmark performance.
  • Long-context is becoming operationally complex: providers with clearer lifecycle contracts and migration tooling may gain enterprise trust.
  • Vector/KG vendors need ops credibility: cache/TTL/export/schema/token-accounting issues affect production adoption as much as retrieval metrics.
  • Informational only; no direct buy/sell recommendation.
